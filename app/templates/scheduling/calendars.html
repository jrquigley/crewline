Add your calendar

Google Calendar

On a computer, open Google Calendar. Beside Other calendars choose +, then From URL. Paste your private calendar link and add it. It will appear on phones using the same Google account.

iPhone / Apple Calendar

Open Settings, then Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar. Paste the private link. Menus may vary by iOS version.

Android

Add the link to Google Calendar using From URL on a computer. In the Google Calendar app on your phone, enable that calendar and its sync option.

Outlook / other calendars

Choose Add calendar, then Subscribe from web or Subscribe by URL. Paste the private subscription link.

A downloaded calendar file is a snapshot. Use a subscription for changing schedules. Check this site for the latest shift times.

Add Crewline to my phone

iPhone

In Safari, open Crewline, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

In Chrome, open Crewline, open the menu, then choose Add to Home screen.

Your schedule still needs an internet connection. Private pages aren’t saved for offline use.
