# Using Crewline

## Managers

1. Choose **Create event**. Enter its name, location, start and finish; pick the initial department and skill. Overnight Events can finish on the following date.
2. Open each setup, Event and closing shift and choose a person. The list explains availability/skill conflicts. Save leaves a private draft. Add another coverage group for more people, skills or departments. Adjust times or split a long shift under the extra options.
3. Check **Staff hours** and any coverage messages. Use **Review & publish**, then **Publish schedule**. Staff now see this revision; enabled emails queue for affected staff. An open position may publish so eligible staff can request it.
4. Use **Requests** to approve time off or an open shift. Approved open shifts still need Event publication. Approving time off immediately reopens intersecting coverage and tells the staff member the decision.
5. In **Staff**, add/edit memberships and skills. An administrator can grant “Can manage schedules”; manager scope follows that person's departments/locations. Create a private sign-in link and share it securely, or send via configured SMTP. Never share one account among people.
6. To end employment, open the staff record and **End employment and access**, review the consequences, then confirm. Existing access stops and future shifts reopen. Use retained records/history for past schedules. Rehire is an administrator/future workflow, not a switch to restore old tokens.

**Settings** holds coverage, timezone, visibility and weekly email choices. **Activity** shows revisions and delivery status. If an email says review, check the mail provider before retrying. Search and filters help larger schedules; `/` focuses search when available.

## Staff on a phone

Open your manager's private link once to choose a password, then sign in with your email. **Home** shows the next shift, upcoming work, **Request time off** and **Pick up a shift**. **Schedule** lists your published work; the team link shows coworkers if allowed. **Me** contains phone/email details, unavailable times, calendar/phone help and password change.

Time off and open shifts need manager approval. Your schedule changes when a manager publishes, except approved time off removes affected work immediately. New availability blocks flag existing conflicts for your manager; they do not silently cancel assigned work.

To change email, enter the new address and current password, then verify the email you receive. Your existing sign-in address stays active until verification. Ask the administrator if email transport is not set up.

In **Calendar & phone**, create a private subscription link and follow your calendar's instructions. A subscription refreshes; downloading an .ics file once does not. Never share your private link. Replace/revoke it if exposed. Google/Apple control refresh timing, so check the website for urgent changes. Add-to-home-screen makes a shortcut; it does not subscribe a calendar or install a native app.

If you cannot sign in, use Forgot password when email is configured or contact your manager for a fresh private link. Too many failed attempts require waiting 15 minutes. An ended employee account cannot sign in; managers keep its historical records.
